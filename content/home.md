# A closer look at galaxies.

I am an associate professor in the Department of Astronomy at Yonsei University. My research focuses on the physical properties of active galactic nuclei and their host galaxies, as well as the evolution of nearby galaxies. I am also actively involved in the SPHEREx and PRIMA space missions.

## Across galaxies and cosmic time

- **Active galactic nuclei** — Black hole growth, variability, and the physical structures at the hearts of galaxies.
- **Nearby galaxies** — Deep imaging, stellar populations, and the connection between galaxies and their central black holes.
- **Infrared surveys** — SPHEREx and PRIMA, connecting space missions with the study of galaxies and AGNs.
